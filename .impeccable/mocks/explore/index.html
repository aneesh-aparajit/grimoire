Round 3 · minimal

Assigned by the roll

Swiss Grid

Black on near-white with one red: the caret, the current heading's line number and failures. A strict 12-column grid with hairline rules only, no boxes. Line numbers hang in a margin beside the note headings. On the landing page, the wordmark is the poster.
Impeccable's pick

Quiet Instrument

Braun product graphics. Warm-grey panels set into a body, controls that look like soft physical keys, and tiny labels. One orange key means Run. Status is shown by small green or red lights. The risk is that it's familiar: calm, but close to many modern tools.
Competitive alternate

Plate Section

A design annual's plate pages. The source is printed as a plate with registration crosshairs at its corners and a hairline credits row underneath. A pale lavender seal names the source and sits half off the plate's corner. One grotesque face for everything.

Round 2

Assigned by the roll

Library Binding

Each item gets its own buckram cloth, which it keeps in the list and in the study view, with gilt-stamped titles. The pane splitter is the book's spine, with the title stamped down it. A satin ribbon marks where you are in your notes. On the landing page the reading list is a shelf of spines that lift when you hover them.
Competitive alternate

Concourse Signs

Airport wayfinding. A single yellow overhead sign tells you where you are and where you can go, with a monumental item number and black pictogram squares. Yellow is used for wayfinding only. Runs post to a split-flap departures board.
Competitive alternate

Course Map

An orienteering map. The source is the terrain and is never edited. Your notes are the purple course on top: each heading is a numbered control, linked by a line to the part of the source it's about. On the landing page your study history is a course drawn over a contour map.

Round 1

Assigned by the roll

Riso Study Zine

Two-drum risograph print: a blue shell band, yellow marker for highlights and selection, and green only where blue overprints yellow, which means a pass. Fluorescent pink is failure. Grain and slight misregistration on the band and headings only.
Competitive alternate

Lexicon and Plate

A reference book's page. Black text on a fixed measure, guide words in the head rule naming the headings in view, a shoulder index, and colour confined to bordered plates: the source is Plate 14, the sandbox is a figure.
Competitive alternate

Night Chart

A star atlas for late sessions. Navy ground and chalk text, with night-vision red for the open item, annotations and failure. On the landing page the reading list is plotted as a live sky chart, with stars sized by notes and lines joining shared tags.
