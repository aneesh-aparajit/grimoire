Scaled dot-product attention

Divide by √d_k so the softmax doesn't saturate when the dimension grows. Try it on a toy matrix before trusting it.

w = np.exp(q @ k.T / np.sqrt(4))
print((w / w.sum(1, keepdims=True)).round(2)[0])
