3.2.1 Scaled Dot-Product Attention

We call our particular attention "Scaled Dot-Product Attention". The input consists of queries and keys of dimension dk, and values of dimension dv.

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QKᵀ / √dk) V

We suspect that for large values of dk, the dot products grow large in magnitude…

Excerpt: Vaswani et al., 2017

E-01

Why divide by √d_k

Large dot products push softmax into flat gradients. Scaling keeps the variance near 1.
